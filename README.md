# Zafa International | Loan Risk Assessment & Financial Eligibility Analysis

## 📌 Overview

**Zafa International's Loan Risk Assessment & Financial Eligibility Analysis** is a data-driven financial assessment solution designed to evaluate an applicant's financial capacity and potential loan repayment risk.

The system analyzes key financial factors, including **income, requested loan amount, rent expenses, existing debt, financial obligations, and repayment capacity**. These factors are evaluated using financial ratios and predefined risk indicators to provide a clear and understandable assessment.

The solution categorizes applicants into:

* 🟢 **Green — Lower / Manageable Risk**
* 🔴 **Red — Higher Risk**

The project demonstrates how structured financial data can be transformed into meaningful risk indicators to support more informed lending and financial assessment processes.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this solution is to provide a structured approach for analyzing an applicant's financial position before considering a loan.

The system is designed to help assess:

* Estimated repayment capacity

---

## 🔍 Assessment Methodology

The system evaluates the relationship between an applicant's income and their financial commitments.

For example, an applicant with a relatively low monthly income requesting a significantly large loan may represent a higher financial risk if the expected repayment is not compatible with their available income.

Similarly, an applicant with sufficient income, manageable expenses, and a reasonable loan requirement may demonstrate a stronger repayment capacity.

The assessment is therefore based on the **overall financial relationship between income, obligations, loan requirements, and repayment capacity**, rather than on a single financial value.

## 📐 Financial Ratio Analysis

The solution can utilize financial ratios to evaluate the applicant's financial position.

One important measure is the **Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)**:

**DTI = Monthly Debt Obligations ÷ Monthly Income × 100**

A higher ratio generally indicates that a greater portion of income is committed to debt obligations, which may indicate increased repayment risk.

The assessment can also incorporate rent and other recurring expenses to provide a broader view of the applicant's available financial capacity.

---

## 🚦 Risk Indicators

### 🟢 Green — Lower / Manageable Risk

A Green classification indicates that the applicant's financial obligations appear manageable relative to their income and calculated repayment capacity under the project's defined assessment criteria.

### 🔴 Red — Higher Risk

A Red classification indicates that the applicant's financial obligations, requested loan, or repayment requirements may be relatively high compared with their available financial capacity.
